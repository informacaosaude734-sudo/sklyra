How It's Made

From the dock to your basket.

Chapter One

Gulf-caught, same day.

We buy whole fish off local boats every morning. If it wasn't swimming in the Gulf this week, it's not on our board.

Chapter Two

Butchered and breaded in-house.

No pre-portioned mystery fillets. Every crab cake is hand-formed, every fillet hand-cut, right behind the counter.

Chapter Three

Fried to order, every basket.

Nothing sits under a heat lamp. Your order hits the oil after you place it — that's why it's worth the short wait.

Chapter Four

Served fast, eaten faster.

Counter service, real plates, no pretense. Most orders are up in under ten minutes flat.

Our Story

Built by people who actually fish.

The Fish Guy started as a cooler on the back of a boat and turned into the counter on Grand Blvd everyone in New Port Richey knows by name. We still buy whole and butcher in-house — no frozen shortcuts, no mystery fillets.
